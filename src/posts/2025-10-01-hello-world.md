# Hello World

This is my first blog post about the **Train RTS** project! I'm excited to share my journey building this real-time strategy game.

## What is Train RTS?

Train RTS is a real-time strategy game where players build and manage railway networks. The game features:

- Dynamic train routing
- Resource management
- Multiplayer support
- Beautiful graphics
